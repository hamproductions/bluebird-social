While the news of the Sendai satellite's closure hit Shion hard early in the month, the bond between members turned it into a story of resilience and positive choice. L-High Culture Festival in Akihabara!

Held on Feb 14-15 at Otonokizaka Academy's old auditorium. It featured the first live performance with all 10 members, Miracle Kanazawa's sweets sales, and Mai Azabu's app distribution, serving as the culmination of their online and real-world activities.
