Monthly Tweet Summary

TL;DR

In August, Ikizulive held its first in-person gathering in Osaka, achieving the major goal of a street live performance. Monthly Highlights

In August, Ikizulive finally realized their long-awaited in-person gathering in Osaka, with all 10 members uniting on the grand stage of a street live performance to deliver a moving show. Each member experienced growth through auditions, birthdays, trips, and daily activities, further strengthening their bond as a group, making it a month filled with hope to overcome their "Ikizurai" (difficult to live) days.
