Monthly Tweet Summary

TL;DR

The Ikizulive Club finally held its 'First Real-World Gathering' in Osaka, performing their first street live to great success! It was an unforgettable summer month where their online bonds truly came to life.

Monthly Highlights

August was a historic turning point for the Ikizulive Club. During their 'Osaka Expedition,' all 10 members met physically for the first time, strengthening their bonds through takoyaki parties and sleepovers. Beyond the success of their street live performance, individual members also took on 'grand adventures' like auditions and solo trips, showing significant personal growth. With the 'Culture Festival' now in their sights, the club's momentum is unstoppable.

First Real-World Gathering in Osaka

On August 14-15, members from all over Japan gathered in Osaka. All 10 stayed at Aurora Konohana's house, talking through the night and practicing hard. It was the moment their online club became 'real.'
